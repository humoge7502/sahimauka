Privacy Policy — DastakDe

Effective: 2026-11-01 · No account. No analytics. No personal data sent to our servers.

What the app does

DastakDe shows verified student opportunities — hackathons, internships, scholarships, fellowships, entrance-exam notices and government schemes — collected from official public sources. Every item links to its official page. The app also lets you save items, set deadline reminders and refresh the feed.

Data the app accesses

  • No personal data leaves your device. There is no sign-up, no account, no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.
  • Saved items, tracked categories, tour state and reminder settings are stored only in app-local storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.
  • Network requests are limited to downloading the public feed JSON from humoge7502.github.io/sahimauka and opening official opportunity or report links in your browser. These requests carry no account identifier or device ID added by us.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device (deadline reminders and an optional 09:00 digest). The app does not use a remote push service.
  • Background refresh, when enabled by Android, re-downloads the public feed and updates local new-opportunity alerts. It does not upload anything about you.

Permissions and why

PermissionPurpose
InternetDownload the public feed and open official links.
NotificationsDeadline reminders and the optional daily digest. You can disable them at any time.
Background work (WorkManager)Periodically refresh the public feed on Android so new opportunities are not missed. No personal data is transmitted.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage-wide file access, SMS or call permissions.

Third-party sites

When you open an opportunity, you leave DastakDe and visit the organiser's or government's website. That site has its own privacy policy. DastakDe does not receive information about what you do there.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for students and collects no personal information from any user, including children under 13.

Changes

Material changes ship with an app update and update the effective date above. We do not retroactively change how existing local data is handled.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/humoge7502/sahimauka/issues. Please do not include personal information in a public issue.
