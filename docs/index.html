Trust is the product

How an opportunity gets in

No scraped mystery meat, no pay-to-apply rows. The pipeline is small enough to audit, and every item carries the evidence behind it.

  1. Collect from official sources. Devpost API, Devfolio listings, NTA notices, CBSE circulars, PIB education releases and registered public programmes.
  2. Verify. Domain allowlist, deadline sanity in IST, source URL, and a scam filter for registration-fee and pay-to-apply patterns.
  3. Exclude, don't guess. Administrative notices, results, answer keys and expired items are dropped. A flagged item never reaches the feed.
  4. Show the deadline honestly. Fixed dates get a countdown; rolling and "check the portal" programmes are labelled as such, not given an invented date.
  5. Remind the student. Local notifications at D-7, D-3, D-1 and D0 for saved items; a daily digest at 09:00 for new matches.
