From submission to student

How a listing goes live

  1. Submit the official URL. Use the listing form. Tell us the deadline and who can apply.
  2. We verify. We open your official page, check the domain, dates and eligibility, and run the scam filter. If anything is unclear, we ask before publishing.
  3. Invoice, if it is a paid listing. Verification comes first. We send the invoice and publish when it is settled; the first listing for early organisers is free.
  4. Students see it with evidence. The card shows the deadline status, source and verification date. They can save it and set reminders.
  5. You can correct it. Reply on the same issue. We target one business day for corrections or withdrawal.
Eligibility

What we will and will not list

✅ We list

Hackathons, internships, scholarships, fellowships, entrance exams and public schemes with a reachable official page and a real deadline (or an honest “rolling” status).

🚫 We do not list

Pay-to-apply “internships”, guaranteed-selection offers, unverifiable prizes, training fees disguised as registration, or anything asking students for money to be considered.

🏷️ Labelling

Paid listings carry a “Verified” badge and are identified as organiser-submitted in the evidence block. Organic ranking is never changed by payment.

🔒 Data boundary

We do not pass student data to organisers. Reach is measured in aggregate: views, saves and reminder counts — never identities.
